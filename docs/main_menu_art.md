# 山门主界面美术

- 生成方式：内置 imagegen。
- 游戏资源：`assets/backgrounds/mountain_gate.webp`。
- 设计：仙山、云海、山门与石阶；左侧留题字位置、右侧留模式入口位置。界面文字、金边、微光和交互均由 Godot 绘制。
- 以下是实际生成提示词，便于后续保持画风。

An ancient immortal sect mountain gateway, turquoise mist in vast towering karst peaks, distant floating palace and waterfalls, weathered carved stone stairs winding from foreground toward a luminous heavenly mountain sanctuary. Gold dawn light on the central and right mountain ridges, dark teal pines and elegant curved temple roof framing the edges, a few glowing golden motes. Luxurious painterly detail with ink-wash-inspired atmospheric depth, sophisticated Chinese fantasy, adventurous and mysterious, not cute or cartoon.
Composition: on the left third, quiet dark teal mist and shadow for large game title overlay. Right third, darker subdued temple/pine shapes with low contrast to support a vertical menu overlay. Main breathtaking vista in center, strong depth. Wide cinematic composition, full bleed.
Constraints: this is only environment artwork, no people, no letters, no logos, no UI, no borders, no watermark.
